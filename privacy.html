Privacy Policy

Tender is published by Ankur Anwekar. Last updated 20 November 2026. Questions: ankuranwekar.dev@gmail.com.

The short version

Tender has no accounts and no analytics. What you put into it stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and we do not sell, share or use it for advertising, because we never receive it.

What Tender stores, and where

  • Period dates that you enter, or that Tender reads from a photo or screenshot of a calendar that you take or choose. The picture is analysed on your phone, using Apple's on-device text recognition, and is not uploaded or kept by Tender; only the dates it contains are saved.
  • What you type in: a first name, favourites, things to remember, and which ideas you marked done or skipped.
  • Your settings, such as how often ideas arrive and at what time.
  • Ideas you have done, which are listed on the History screen.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app, or choosing Start over inside it, erases it.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to sign in with Apple. Without it, Tender does not contact our servers or anyone else's. Reminders are scheduled on the device. Some ideas include a button that opens Maps or Messages; what happens there is covered by those apps' own policies.

If you pair two phones (available only in editions where pairing is offered): the partner who tracks her cycle chooses what to share. That information is end-to-end encrypted on her phone before it is sent, passes through a relay that stores only ciphertext it cannot read, and is decrypted on her partner's phone. The relay also holds a random mailbox identifier and, if notifications are on, a push token so it can wake the other phone. It holds no name, email address, phone number or location. Relay messages are deleted after 30 days, and unpairing deletes them at once.

If you sign in with Apple (optional)

Signing in is never required. If you do, Tender keeps one encrypted backup of your data so a new or reinstalled phone can pick up where this one left off.

  • It is encrypted on your phone before it is sent. The key stays on your devices (in your iCloud Keychain, and in a recovery code you can write down). Tender and its server cannot read the backup, and cannot recover the key if you lose it.
  • What the server holds: the encrypted backup (and the one before it, kept as a safety copy), its size and the time it last changed, filed under a scrambled form of the identifier Apple gives Tender for you. Tender asks Apple for nothing else: no name, no email address.
  • Deleting it: Profile → Back up → Delete the backup and account erases both copies from the server for good. Signing out keeps it, so you can sign back in later. You can also remove Tender's access in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Health data

If you choose to import from Apple Health or Health Connect, Tender reads cycle records (periods, ovulation tests, temperature) on your device to estimate your cycle. It never writes to them, never uses them for advertising, and never shares them except in the end-to-end encrypted form described above, at the level you choose.

Permissions

  • Notifications, to deliver the daily idea.
  • Calendar, only if you add the Seasons calendar. Tender does not read your other events.
  • Camera, only when you tap Take a photo to photograph a calendar, and to scan a pairing code where pairing is offered. A photo is read on your phone and is not uploaded or saved by Tender.
  • Photos: choosing a photo or screenshot uses the system photo picker, which gives Tender that one image and nothing else.

Children

Tender is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Tender gives estimates for general wellbeing. It is not a medical device, is not a contraceptive, and does not diagnose or treat anything.
