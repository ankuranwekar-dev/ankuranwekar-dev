The screenshot was not recognised

Use a screenshot of the month view of her period app: a grid of day numbers with the period days marked. A cycle wheel or a week strip will not work. If it still fails, choose Pick a date and tap the day her last period started.

The dates are wrong

On the confirmation screen, tap any day to mark or unmark it. If Tender guessed the wrong month, tap the month it offers under the title. You can add a newer screenshot at any time from her name, top right.

Tender asks whether her period started

When Tender only has what you gave it, it cannot know. Tap Yes, Not yet, or pick the actual day, and the seasons adjust.

Notifications are not arriving

Open Settings, then Notifications, then Tender, and allow them. Ideas arrive at the time set under her name.

Deleting everything

Tap her name (top right), scroll to the bottom and choose Start over, or delete the app.

Important

Tender gives estimates. It is not a contraceptive and not a medical device. See the privacy policy.
